|---|---|---|
| SIH26094 = "AI-Powered Dynamic Mental Health Monitoring and Distress Prediction System for **Victims of Atrocities**", MoSJE; beneficiaries under the SC/ST (PoA) Act 1989, registered via NHAA 14566 / Integrated Portal | SIH 2026 problem-statement viewer (sih2026-ps-viewer.vercel.app/ps/SIH26094; data from sih.gov.in) | Primary profile = `atrocity_victims`. | The brief says existing mechanisms focus on legal/financial support, not continuous wellbeing monitoring | same page | The gap Manasetu fills. |
| The brief asks for Emotion AI, voice-stress analytics, predictive risk modelling | same page | **Deliberately not built**: no validated model exists; we use transparent rules + clinician review. Say so openly. |
| e-Prisons (NIC) has PIMS, eVisitor, **eHospital**, legal aid, inventory, PMS modules | eprisons.nic.in/Public/About.aspx | Do NOT claim it lacks medical records. Manasetu is complementary: longitudinal self-reported wellbeing + prioritisation + follow-up. |
| NHAA 14566: docket numbers, online grievance tracking, auto-compliance reminders, state dashboards | nhapoa.gov.in FAQ; PIB-based explainers | Tracks legal compliance, not psychological wellbeing. |
| Tele-MANAS 14416: 24x7, ~20 languages, callbacks/follow-up, links to DMHP | MoHFW / NIMHANS-linked pages | Reactive helpline. Offered as a **referral destination**, not a competitor. |
| Hindi validations exist for K10, PHQ-9 etc. in specific populations | Oxford / other validation papers | Validation is population- and language-specific. **Tamil not verified.** Clinicians must choose the instrument. |

Unverified / needs a human: legal basis for data sharing, ethics approval, consent process for people in custody or in legal proceedings, clinical cut-offs.
